About the show

Jonathan and Marili unpack chemistry, attachment, sex, communication and the patterns we swear we have outgrown. A relationship coach is not immune to being called out, and nobody gets to hide behind knowing better.

Witty, self-aware and a little mischievous: we laugh at our choices and rationalizations, never at people.
